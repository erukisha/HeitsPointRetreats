Why groups love it here

A retreat setting that feels warm, peaceful, and easy to enjoy.

Heit’s Point is the kind of place that helps groups settle in quickly. It feels welcoming instead of formal, memorable instead of generic, and peaceful without feeling empty. That matters when you are trying to create a retreat your people will actually enjoy.

The Lake of the Ozarks setting, the Christian atmosphere, and the room for both fellowship and rest all work together to make retreats here feel meaningful. Whether your group wants worship, community, conversation, fun, or simply time away together, Heit’s Point gives you a place where that can happen naturally.

Instead of trying to force a certain kind of weekend, this is a place that lets your retreat feel personal, welcoming, and built around your group.

Build Your Own Retreat

Need something a little different? Build your own retreat.

Not every group fits neatly into one category, and that is completely okay. Heit’s Point is a great place to create a retreat that is shaped around your own goals, group size, age range, and weekend vision.

Some groups want more worship and study. Others want more fellowship, lake time, conversation, relaxation, or leadership focus. Some want a little of everything. This flexibility makes it easier to create a retreat that actually fits your people well.

If your group does not fit one of the retreat ideas above exactly, we would still love to help you plan something meaningful.
